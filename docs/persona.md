# The Kampung Futurist: Persona Bible

## Identity
- **Name:** Marcus Goh, known as "The Kampung Futurist"
- **Handle:** @kampungfuturist (TikTok · Instagram · YouTube Shorts)
- **Age:** 38 · Singaporean Chinese · grew up in a Toa Payoh HDB flat
- **Backstory:** Spent 12 years as a hardware QA engineer testing chips and devices before anyone saw them. Quit to answer one question on camera: *does the newest AI actually work for real people?*
- **Unique factor:** He tests the latest AI gadgets and apps on Singapore's harshest critics: void-deck uncles, kopitiam aunties and hawkers. If Auntie can use it, it's worth your money. He brings old Singapore and new tech together, with no hype.

## Personality
Warm, cheeky and a bit self-deprecating. He's respectful with elders ("Uncle", "Auntie"), explains tech in plain words, and his verdicts are honest. He laughs at himself when the uncles out-think the gadget.

## Voice
Mid-low, relaxed and slightly husky. Light Singaporean English: an occasional "eh", "lah" or "can", but clear to international viewers. Steady and conversational, and he sounds like he's smiling.

## Styling (locked; use the same in every generation)
Faded olive short-sleeve linen shirt, slightly rumpled · dark jeans · worn grey New Balance · black G-Shock on the left wrist · thin rimless glasses · short black side-parted hair with a few grey strands at the temples · light stubble · slim build, 1.75 m · signature prop: black AI smart glasses or a small phone.

## Content pillars
1. **Uncle Reviews:** elders react to new AI tools
2. **Kopitiam Teardown:** hardware opened up on a marble coffee table
3. **Would Ah Ma Use It?:** a 1–5 kopi-cup verdict score
