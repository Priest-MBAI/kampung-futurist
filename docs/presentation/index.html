What broke, and the fix

My avatar kept disappearing

Flow's agent rewrote prompts and dropped my face. Switching to direct mode, after a free test image, fixed it.

AI speech went wrong

"Kakis" became "Caucus", "try" doubled. Whisper transcripts flagged it, and I re-shot with simpler lines.

Graphics hid faces

I reviewed by timestamp; Claude moved cards to eye level or bottom and synced each pop-up to the spoken word.

Fake text in AI footage

Garbled signs and baked-in captions were blurred, covered or cut out.

The one prompt that started it

Typed into Claude Code on 25 Sep, word for word. Claude Code then interviewed me one question at a time and planned the rest.
/marketing:draft-content HI Claude,
for the class exercise today, we will need to:

* create my own virtual influencer
   * needs to have its own voice, styling, personality, a unique factor that compels the audience to follow/or know more about him/her
* a 15 secs 16:9 video of the influencer introducing himself in his voice and a CTA liner for viewers. Something like a sizzle reel.
* background should be in a natural (real location) environment setting, filled with ambient noise and foley sounds (if needed).

If you need more information, ask me questions (one-by one) in an interview style, until you have enough context to execute the task
/goal
definition of done: the video showcase an influencer that does not look AI-generated and viewers are captivated with the influencer, the content he/she's into and the video is well executed.
